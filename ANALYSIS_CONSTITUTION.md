# PinkOps Analysis Constitution

1. We never invent data.

2. Raw competition data is read-only.

3. Player identity is based on player_id, never player_name.

4. Every derived metric must have a documented definition.

5. Every threshold must be justified or sensitivity-tested.

6. Small samples must receive uncertainty treatment.

7. No player is selected because of one metric alone.

8. AI suggestions are hypotheses, not evidence.

9. All numerical results must be reproducible in Python.

10. Future information must not leak into delivery-level decision variables.

11. Every final recommendation must be traceable to underlying data.

12. Every major modeling choice must survive robustness testing.

13. If a metric cannot be reliably calculated from the data, it must remain unknown rather than being fabricated.

14. The final 20 players must be defensible from both statistical evidence and cricket context.
