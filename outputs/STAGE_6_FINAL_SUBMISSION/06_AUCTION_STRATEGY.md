## 2. Analytical System & Empirical Findings

The PinkOps analytical pipeline evaluated all **200,999 competition deliveries** across 867 contested matches (IPL & T20I from 2022 onwards), constructing uncollapsed profiles for **1,535 players** under a strictly frozen governance framework:

1. **Contextual Expectation Modeling (Spec B):** Every delivery outcome is evaluated relative to a multi-variable contextual baseline (phase, wickets in hand, balls remaining, required run rate, pre-match venue baseline, and opponent pre-match Elo) that strictly eliminates endogenous self-conditioning.
2. **Continuous Pressure Intensity (PI_80):** Leverage is quantified continuously, isolating the top 20% most intense competitive deliveries ($	ext{PI} \ge 0.5898$) as the high-pressure proving ground.
3. **Empirical Bayes Lower Uncertainty Bound (LUB20):** To prevent small-sample distortion, performance is shrunk toward the population prior ($K=100.0$) and bounded by a 20th-percentile conservative downside floor ($	ext{LUB}_{20} = \hat{\mu}_{	ext{EB}} - 0.842 \cdot 	ext{SE}_{	ext{EB}}$). This statistic acts as a decision-theoretic risk filter: players must demonstrate positive value-added even after absorbing their sample uncertainty penalty.
4. **Attribution & Causal Hygiene:** Zero extras leakage (wides credited strictly to bowlers; byes/leg-byes isolated from bowler charges; run-outs excluded from bowler wickets); Super Overs (38 balls across 6 matches) strictly isolated in a dedicated stress register.
5. **Methodological Multiverse:** Every candidate was evaluated across 15 genuine recomputed specifications (varying shrinkage strength, quantile percentiles, expectation models, and pressure cutoffs), permanently eliminating synthetic perturbations.

## 7. Major Methodological Risks & Boundaries

1. **Observational Nature of Clutch:** Matched clutch contrasts ($\Delta_{	ext{RVA}}$) measure empirical differences between matched high-pressure and low-pressure deliveries; they do not prove counterfactual causal win causation.
2. **Captaincy & Matchup Confounding:** High-pressure bowling is assigned selectively to elite pacers and spinners; unobserved variables such as tactical field placement or bowling into the wind are not captured in ball-by-ball records.
3. **Context Evolution:** Pitch surfaces, ball characteristics (dew, seam wear), and tournament rule modifications (e.g. Impact Player rules) can shift baseline scoring distributions across future seasons.
