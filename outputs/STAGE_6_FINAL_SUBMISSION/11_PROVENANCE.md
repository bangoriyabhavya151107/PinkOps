## 1. Explicit Rule Verification & Epistemic Boundaries

To ensure complete transparency, every claim made in the PinkOps submission is categorized into three strict epistemic classes:

### A. VERIFIED BY OFFICIAL COMPETITION RULES
1. **Submission Requirements:** Two ordered top-10 lists (10 best-performing batters, 10 best-performing bowlers) selected from Rank 1 to Rank 10 on Unstop dropdowns, accompanied by a comprehensive methodology document.
2. **Data Scope:** Historical IPL and T20 International data from 2022 onwards, comprising delivery-level ball-by-ball records, matches, competitions, teams, and playing XIs.
3. **Pressure Definition:** Explicitly delegated to participants to formulate scientifically from match context (RRR, wickets in hand, balls remaining, phase, close contests).
4. **Anti-Reputation Mandate:** Rankings must be supported by empirical analysis of supplied data, not reputation alone.
5. **Fair Comparisons:** Mandatory treatment of sample size variation, context normalization, and IPL vs T20I differences.

### B. PINKOPS ANALYTICAL DECISIONS
1. **Specification B Expectation Model:** Contextual multi-variable baseline eliminating endogenous 12-ball self-conditioning.
2. **Continuous Pressure Formulation (PI_80):** Continuous Win-Probability Pressure Index thresholded at the 80th percentile ($\text{PI} \ge 0.5898$).
3. **Empirical Bayes Shrinkage & LUB20:** Prior shrinkage ($K=100.0$) and 20th-percentile lower uncertainty bounds to prevent small-sample distortion.
4. **Super Over Isolation:** Quarantining 38 Super Over balls into a stress register; excluding them from regular 20-over expectation baselines.
5. **Multiverse Robustness Grid:** Evaluating all qualified players across 15 genuine recomputed branches.
6. **Tactical Role Portfolio Structure:** Allocating 10 batters across 3 roles (3 Top-Order, 3 Middle, 4 Death) and 10 bowlers across 3 roles with a 50/50 pace/spin split.
