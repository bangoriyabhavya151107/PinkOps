### Tactical Rationale for Distribution
T20 matches are disproportionately won or lost at the death (overs 16–20) where leverage spikes exponentially. Allocating 4 spots to verified death finishers ensures the franchise always has multiple specialist options in high-pressure chases, while 3 middle accelerators and 3 powerplay anchors maintain structural stability.
